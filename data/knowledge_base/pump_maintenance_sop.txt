PUMP MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION PROCEDURE

Document ID: SOP-MECH-PUMP-001
Revision: 03
Department: Mechanical Maintenance
Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE

This procedure defines the inspection and maintenance requirements
for centrifugal process pumps used in industrial service.

2. BASE PLATE AND FOUNDATION INSPECTION

During routine inspection, the pump base plate and foundation shall
be examined for corrosion, cracking, loose fasteners, deterioration,
or other visible abnormalities.

Minor surface corrosion shall be cleaned and assessed to determine
whether the affected area is superficial or requires further
engineering evaluation.

Significant corrosion, section loss, cracking, or deterioration
affecting structural integrity shall be reported to the responsible
maintenance or reliability engineer before further operation or
maintenance work is authorized.

3. MECHANICAL SEAL INSPECTION

The mechanical seal area shall be checked for visible leakage,
moisture, abnormal deposits, or other indications of seal
degradation.

Persistent leakage or evidence of seal failure shall be referred
to Mechanical Maintenance for detailed inspection and corrective
action.

4. COUPLING AND GUARD

The coupling guard shall be installed, secure, and free from
damage.

Equipment shall not be operated with a missing or materially
damaged coupling guard unless an approved maintenance procedure
specifically permits the condition.

5. INSTRUMENTATION

Pressure and temperature indicators associated with the pump shall
be checked for physical condition and valid calibration status.

Where calibration status cannot be established, the instrument
record shall be verified before relying on the measurement for
engineering decisions.

6. PRESSURE SAFETY DEVICES

Pressure safety valves associated with the pumping system shall
have valid inspection, testing, and certification records.

The installed set pressure shall be verified against the approved
design documentation.

Relief discharge routing shall not be altered without appropriate
engineering approval.

7. INSPECTION RECORDS

Inspection findings shall identify the equipment tag, observed
condition, evidence available, recommended action, and any
uncertainty requiring field verification.

Safety-critical conclusions shall not be based solely on visual
inspection or automated document analysis.

8. ESCALATION

Conditions that may affect equipment integrity, personnel safety,
pressure containment, or reliable operation shall be escalated to
the responsible engineering or reliability function.

END OF PROCEDURE
